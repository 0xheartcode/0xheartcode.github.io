PROJECT OVERVIEW

An NFT marketplace contract suite for SOLO Chain that handles ERC721, ERC1155 and ERC6909 tokens in a single marketplace, a rare breadth that spans classic NFTs, multi-tokens, and the newer minimal multi-token standard. It applies safe-transfer patterns throughout to protect assets across the listing and settlement paths, and is covered by a Foundry test suite exercising the trading flows.
