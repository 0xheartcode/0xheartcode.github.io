PROJECT OVERVIEW

A cross-chain bridge that moves ERC721 and ERC1155 tokens from other chains onto SOLO Chain, representing them as ERC6909. It integrates with MetaLayer for cross-chain messaging and is source-domain-aware, so the origin chain is accounted for when minting the bridged representation, an important safety property for any bridge. It is covered by a Foundry test suite exercising the bridge paths.
