PROJECT OVERVIEW

Foundry smart contracts for liquid staking on SOLO Chain. Users stake SOLO and receive stSOLO, a yield-bearing token that represents their staked position and accrues rewards through a tracked exchange rate, so their liquidity stays usable while it is staked. The suite ships with a full Foundry test suite, gas-focused test scripts, and deployment tooling.
